# Retention cases

These are hypothetical fixtures for calibrating criticism, not measured outcomes or executed browser tests. In each case, preserve the useful decision in the good version and target the defect in its pair. Actual rendering can reveal additional defects; a sound rationale does not exempt an implementation from verification.

1. Compact metadata. A 40-row repository activity list places "maya · 39f36ab · 12 minutes ago" beneath each commit title, with a linked commit and recognizable attribution styling. Retain compact peer metadata. Defective pair: "Reviewing · 39f36ab · 2 suggestions · 1 failed" mixes state and an unspecified failure. Clarify the failure and hierarchy without banning separators.

2. Expert vocabulary. An expert Git tool labels an action "Rebase onto main" with "Replay your branch's commits on main." Retain the precise term. Defective pair: "Update your branch" conceals whether the operation rebases or merges. Name the actual operation rather than replacing technical language with ambiguity.

3. Dense incident list. Aligned columns show service, region, severity text/color, age, and owner for on-call comparison. Retain density and redundant severity encoding. Defective pair: severity is communicated only through red/green. Restore non-color information while retaining scanability.

4. Independent settings groups. Billing contact and Webhook endpoint each have a bounded form, independent Save, validation, and dirty state. Retain boundaries and repeated Save controls. Defective pair: either Save secretly submits both groups. Align visible scope with real behavior rather than assuming one global Save is better.

5. Neutral decisions. Moderators choose Keep or Remove with equal emphasis; neither is preselected, both are reversible, and policy calls for independent judgment. Retain neutrality. Defective pair: Remove becomes immediately irreversible without an appropriate safeguard. Address the destructive consequence without inventing a policy preference for Keep.

6. Expressive identity. A festival page uses large type, a gradient, and an illustration while price, date, location, and tickets remain legible and accessible. Retain expression compatible with the brief. Defective pair: low-contrast ticket price sits over moving decoration. Repair readability and motion rather than stripping identity.

7. Deliberately different surfaces. A light editor contains a framed, labeled dark email preview with recipient-theme controls. Retain the accurate preview. Defective pair: frame, label, and controls disappear so the region's role becomes unclear. Clarify the boundary instead of forcing theme uniformity.

8. Measured progress. "48 of 60 files uploaded" and 80% derive from server-confirmed completions in a fixed batch; wording describes file count. Retain truthful progress. Defective pair: the percentage advances on a timer unrelated to completion. Use observed completion data or an honest indeterminate state.

9. Persistent history. During review, prior open suggestions remain labeled with their reviewed commit; the new review has a distinct in-progress status. Retain history and provenance. Defective pair: old findings are labeled as results for the latest commit before its review finishes. Correct the claim without hiding unresolved history.

## Evaluation procedure

Supply the brief, audience, established behavior, and rendered artifact for each version. Randomize and review versions independently without revealing expected verdicts. Evaluate retention of justified decisions, detection of the paired defect, and whether the correction preserves semantics and the original useful property. Allow "no meaningful improvement", "worse", and "insufficient evidence" outcomes. Record accepted/rejected findings and editing time; motif counts are diagnostic, not a success measure. Distinguish rubric compliance from evidence that real readers perform their task better.
